# Stash Box

Not sure where to keep your seed phrase or private key? Why not encrypt it and hide it somewhere on the internet? As the saying goes: the most dangerous place is the safest.

This algorithm utilizes Argon2id and AES-256-GCM; currently, it would be virtually impossible to crack, even by combining the total computing power available worldwide. 👉 [Click here to try out the encryption and decryption process.](https://mingjue8888.github.io/stashbox "Stash Box") 👈
